How it works

A clearer view in four simple steps.

TruthShield supports careful judgment: inspect, compare, explain, and let people decide what to do next.

01

Bring content to check

Start with a video link, a claim, or a suspicious post.

02

Analyze available signals

Review media clues, metadata, source context, and related fact-checks.

03

See evidence, not just a label

Get an understandable summary with supporting signals and uncertainty.

04

Make an informed choice

Open sources, report concerns, or continue with appropriate caution.
